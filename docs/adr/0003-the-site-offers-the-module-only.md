# The public site offers the DNL-N6 and no DevKit

Home, Products, and the DNL-N6 page stay three pages. Home introduces the Brand and leads to the module and to Contact. Products is the catalog, one row until there is a second Product. The DNL-N6 page is for an engineer who will design a Carrier. It states the public facts, names what is still Open, and collects Interest. It shows no price and no buy button.

The DevKit is not a Product. The site has no DevKit page, catalog row, related-product link, viewer tab, or form choice. An engineer who wants an eval carrier says so in the message.

## Considered Options

- Keep the DevKit as a second Product. That offers an eval board the site is not ready to sell.
- Drop Products and let Home be the module page. The next module would have nowhere to land except a redesign.

## Consequences

Blog and About stay. A sentence that would link to a DevKit page is repaired. Consulting, Docs, thank-you, and the 404 stay. Checks that require a DevKit link or a DevKit form choice change with the pages.

The Palette, Brand green, and the Mark, Wordmark, and Logo files stay. The site typeface and the display sizes of those drawings are for the design session. Every reworked page has to be usable on a phone: one column, the same facts as on a wide screen, a header lockup that fits.
