The DNL-N6 is the first Dino’s Lab module. It is in design. Nothing here is a datasheet yet.

The target is a small system-on-module built around the **STM32N6**: Arm Cortex-M55 at up to 800 MHz, ST Neural-ART accelerator (up to 600 GOPS), 4.2 MB of on-chip SRAM, MIPI CSI-2 camera path, and an H.264 encoder. That is the silicon. The module is our packaging of it — a board you can drop onto a carrier, in the same spirit as a RoadRunner SOM, aimed at cameras, inspection, and other edge-AI jobs that do not need a full Linux MPU.

What we will publish when the design is real:

- Pinout and mechanical drawing
- Power and boot
- Camera and memory options
- Bring-up notes and example firmware
